
# Crave — Feed your passion

### Path Title
Developing with Data: Lists, Searching, and Sorting

### Path Overview
In this path, you will learn how to build an app that feels alive. Instead of building one page at a time, you will learn how to take a list of information and turn it into a beautiful, interactive gallery. You will also build a super-fast search bar and filter buttons that let your users find exactly what they are looking for in seconds. By the end, you'll know how to keep track of a user's favorite items and even save their data to a real database.

### Project Title
Build Crave: A Modern Food App

### Project Narrative
You are going to build Crave, a vibrant and community-driven recipe app. You will start by designing a clean layout that showcases high-quality food photography from a list of recipes. Then, you will make the app interactive by adding a "Like" button and a real-time search tool that updates the screen as you type. Finally, you will learn how to save your favorite recipes into a personal digital box and connect your app to a live database so your data is saved forever.

### Story Arc
Meet Sarah. She is a passionate home cook who is always looking for new inspiration. She has hundreds of bookmarks and messy notes scattered everywhere, making it impossible to find that one "perfect" breakfast recipe she loved last week. She feels overwhelmed by slow, cluttered websites that make cooking feel like a chore instead of a joy.

Sarah dreams of a simple, beautiful place where she can see a stream of fresh meal ideas, quickly search for "vegan" or "pasta," and heart the recipes she wants to try later. She wants her private "Recipe Box" to be clean and organized so she can focus on what she loves most: cooking.

This is where you come in. You are going to build Crave to solve Sarah's problem. You will start by taking a raw list of food ideas and turning them into a stunning gallery of cards. You'll build the tools that let Sarah search and filter her feed instantly. Finally, you'll write the logic that lets her "save" her favorites into her own personal collection. Let's get cooking!


**Stack:** React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Supabase

> **The Big Idea:** Crave is a platform where foodies discover their next meal. It features a searchable, filterable recipe feed, interactive "Like" buttons, category filters, and a personal "Recipe Box" for saved items. It is the perfect React teacher because filtering, searching, and favoriting are the three core pillars of real-world React development.
